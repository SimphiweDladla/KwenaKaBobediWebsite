Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your personal information in accordance with POPIA.

This Privacy Policy complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) of South Africa. Last updated: January 2026.

1. Who We Are

Kwena Ka Bobedi (Pty) Ltd is a South African registered company providing school inventory management services. We act as a Responsible Party under POPIA in respect of personal information we collect through this website and in the course of our service delivery.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, phone number, school name, and role. We may also collect information about your school's enrollment size and province for the purpose of preparing service proposals.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used to: (a) respond to your quote or contact requests; (b) prepare tailored service proposals; (c) communicate updates relevant to your service engagement; and (d) meet our legal obligations.

4. POPIA Compliance

We process personal information lawfully, for specific purposes, and with your explicit consent where required. You have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact our Information Officer at privacy@kwanakabobedi.co.za.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Contact information is retained for a maximum of 3 years from last interaction unless an active service relationship exists.

6. Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with trusted service providers (e.g., email platforms) solely for the purpose of delivering our services, under strict confidentiality obligations.

7. Cookies

This website may use functional cookies to improve your browsing experience. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

8. Contact Our Information Officer

For any POPIA-related enquiries, requests, or complaints, please contact our Information Officer at: privacy@kwanakabobedi.co.za or call +27 (0)11 000 0000.
