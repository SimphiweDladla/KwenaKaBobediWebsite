How It Works

Your Full Academic Year, Managed

Our service follows the South African school calendar from January through December, ensuring continuous coverage at every critical inventory moment.

1
January – February
Onboarding & Baseline Assessment
We conduct an initial site visit to understand your current stock management state, identify gaps, and agree on a tailored service plan. A baseline physical count establishes your opening stock position for the academic year.
2
February – March
System Setup & Staff Orientation
We configure your inventory tracking framework (digital or hybrid), set up item registers, and provide a brief orientation to all relevant staff — from the storeroom administrator to the principal.
3
Term 1 & 2 (April – June)
Active LTSM Issuing & Monitoring
We manage or oversee the issuing of textbooks and stationery to learners, maintaining signed acknowledgement records. Monthly stock reports are produced for the principal and SGB.
4
July (Mid-Year)
Mid-Year Cycle Count & Reconciliation
A full mid-year cycle count reconciles physical stock against system records. Variances are investigated and reported, and adjustments are made with documented justification.
5
Term 3 (July – September)
Ongoing Monitoring & Replenishment
We monitor consumption rates, trigger replenishment requests ahead of stock-outs, and manage supplier deliveries during the second half of the year.
6
Term 4 (October – November)
LTSM Collection & Year-End Prep
We manage the collection of textbooks from learners, assess condition for re-use in the next academic year, and begin preparation of the year-end audit documentation package.
7
November – December
Annual Audit & Year-End Report
A comprehensive year-end physical audit produces the full annual stock report — ready for district submission, SGB review, and the following year's procurement planning.
Why a Year-Round Partner Matters
Most inventory problems in schools don't happen in December — they happen in February when books are rushed out without records, or in July when nobody knows what's missing. Our year-round presence means issues are caught and resolved before they become audit findings.
