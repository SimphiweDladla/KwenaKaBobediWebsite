Our Services

Comprehensive School Inventory Management

Every service we offer is designed around the unique operational pressures of South African public and independent schools.

Service 01

LTSM Stock Management

Learner Teacher Support Material (LTSM) is one of a school's largest budget items. We manage the full lifecycle — from order verification and receiving to issuing to learners and year-end returns.

What's Included
  • ✓Delivery verification against official purchase orders
  • ✓Condition grading and tagging of all books and materials
  • ✓Learner-level issuing records with parent acknowledgement
  • ✓End-of-year collection and condition assessment for reuse analysis
Service 02

Stationery & Consumables Control

Day-to-day stationery is where leakage happens silently. Our controls stop shrinkage at the source through tightly managed issuing processes.

What's Included
  • ✓Monthly requisition and issuing cycles per department
  • ✓Teacher and admin acknowledgement sign-offs
  • ✓Low-stock threshold alerts for timely re-ordering
  • ✓Supplier invoice matching and budget variance reporting
Service 03

Stock Counts & Physical Audits

We conduct scheduled cycle counts and comprehensive year-end physical audits that meet DBE and SGB reporting requirements.

What's Included
  • ✓Scheduled quarterly cycle counts
  • ✓Full annual physical stocktake
  • ✓Variance identification and root-cause analysis
  • ✓Audit-ready documentation packages for district submission
Service 04

Reconciliation & Reporting

Accurate reconciliation between physical stock and recorded balances is the foundation of financial accountability.

What's Included
  • ✓System-to-physical reconciliation reports
  • ✓Unexplained variance investigation and write-off process
  • ✓Month-end management reports for principals and SGBs
  • ✓Year-end financial summary aligned to NSNP and procurement records
