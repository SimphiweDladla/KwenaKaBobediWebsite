Our Core Services

Everything Schools Need to Stay Accountable

From receiving and issuing to full annual audits, we manage the entire inventory lifecycle so educators can focus on teaching.

📦
Stock Receiving & Verification
We receive, verify, and capture all incoming LTSM and stationery deliveries against official orders, ensuring zero discrepancies from the outset.
📋
Issuing & Distribution Control
Controlled issuing processes with signature trails ensure every item distributed to educators, classes, or departments is fully accounted for.
🔍
Stock Counts & Reconciliation
Scheduled cycle counts and year-end reconciliations give you a clear picture of balances, usage, and any unexplained variances.
📊
Audit Trail Management
Digital and paper-based audit trails are maintained in compliance with DBE and POPIA requirements, ready for district inspections.
🖥️
Remote Support & Reporting
Our remote service tier provides monthly stock reports, variance alerts, and principal dashboards without requiring on-site visits every week.
📅
Annual Academic Cycle Planning
We plan your full inventory calendar — from pre-year procurement to end-of-year surplus returns — aligned to your school's academic calendar.

Our Services

Comprehensive School Inventory Management

Every service we offer is designed around the unique operational pressures of South African public and independent schools.

📦
Service 01

LTSM Stock Management

Learner Teacher Support Material (LTSM) is one of a school's largest budget items. We manage the full lifecycle — from order verification and receiving to issuing to learners and year-end returns.

What's Included
  • ✓Delivery verification against official purchase orders
  • ✓Condition grading and tagging of all books and materials
  • ✓Learner-level issuing records with parent acknowledgement
  • ✓End-of-year collection and condition assessment for reuse analysis
🖊️
Service 02

Stationery & Consumables Control

Day-to-day stationery is where leakage happens silently. Our controls stop shrinkage at the source through tightly managed issuing processes.

What's Included
  • ✓Departmental and classroom allowance tracking
  • ✓Reorder point alerts before stock runs out
  • ✓Bulk storage organization and labeling
